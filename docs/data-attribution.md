# Data attribution

Every dataset is real, public and free to download. None is redistributed in this repository (`data/` is git-ignored); only small derived model files and summary numbers are committed. The one synthetic thing is the Fleet page's **sample fleet** (60 invented buses across 3 depots, generated in `frontend/src/lib/fleet/sample.ts`): it exists only to show the page working, is labelled "Synthetic, for demonstration" wherever it appears, and is never used to fit or check a model. No model figure rests on it. The app's ageing models are NREL's (see below); the HUST, Naumann and Stanford rows describe our own laboratory fit, which is kept in the code but no longer shown. Licences are those stated by each source at download time; check the source before reusing a dataset.

Notes on use
- The Stanford series has no declared licence. The project uses it for a non-commercial course assignment and does not republish it. Anyone reusing the project beyond that should contact the data's authors.
- EVBattery's authors state that values were perturbed, and its capacity labels are derived rather than measured. The chemistry is undisclosed and the cell voltage reaches 4.29 V, so it is treated as NMC-like and is never used to fit the LFP models.
- **NREL BLAST-Lite** (https://github.com/NatLabRockies/BLAST-Lite, version 1.1.3; previously github.com/NREL/BLAST-Lite) is the ageing engine of the app. `engine/reference/` is a Python port of the equations and parameters of its `Lfp_Gr_250AhPrismatic` and `Lfp_Gr_SonyMurata3Ah_Battery` models, used under the BSD-3-Clause licence: Copyright (c) Alliance for Energy Innovation, LLC. The licence text and attribution are kept in `engine/reference/NOTICE`. (PyPI lists Apache-2.0; the BSD-3 text shipped in the package is the one reproduced.) The models were fitted by NREL and its collaborators to the laboratory data cited on the Evidence page (Sony-Murata 3 Ah: TUM tests including the Naumann storage set listed above; 250 Ah prismatic: NREL tests). This project did not re-fit them. `scripts/crosscheck_blast.py` runs the package itself offline, in a separate environment, to produce the outputs the port is tested against. The package is not an app dependency.
- Per-dataset provenance (checksum, row counts, known limitations) is in `engine/data/provenance/*.json` and is served by the app's `/evidence` endpoint.
- `frontend/public/samples/sample-fleet-duty-ILLUSTRATIVE.csv` is a made-up example for trying the CSV import. It is not real fleet data and is never used for training.

- **Fonts in the PDF report**: Lexend (Copyright The Lexend Project Authors) and Manrope (Copyright The Manrope Project Authors) are used under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, the same families the app shows. Static instances of the variable fonts are kept in `backend/app/fonts/` with the licence texts (`OFL-Lexend.txt`, `OFL-Manrope.txt`); they are used unmodified in design, only converted from variable to static files.

- **Electricity tariff (Maharashtra, Pune)**: read from the Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission's order in Case No. 75 of 2025 (post-remand), 25 March 2026, Annexure II, and its time-of-day Table 8 (paras 18.13 and 18.14). FY 2026-27, in force from 1 April 2026: HT IX (HT EV charging station) energy ₹8.92 + wheeling ₹0.81 per kVAh (page 100 of 123; EHV supply has no wheeling charge); LT VIII (LT EV charging station) energy ₹7.73 + wheeling ₹1.52 per kVAh (page 95), no fixed or demand charge for either. Time of day applies to the energy charge only: 00:00 to 09:00 0 % (the night rebate is removed), 09:00 to 17:00 a rebate of 15 % (April to September) or 25 % (October to March), 17:00 to 24:00 +20 %. The app uses the average of the two solar-hour rebates (20 %). The order's footnote for the peak rate did not come through in the text extraction; the +20 % for EV charging is taken from Note 3 to the ToD table in MERC's press note on the earlier MYT order (Case No. 217 of 2024, https://merc.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Press-Note_MSEDCL-MYT-Order_English.pdf), which applies it to every category except residential, group housing and agriculture. That order's own EV rates (₹8.02 for LT VIII and ₹8.46 for HT IX in FY 2025-26) were revised by the March 2026 order and are not used. Not included: electricity duty, the fuel adjustment charge, tax on sale of electricity, power-factor penalties, and tariff changes after FY 2026-27. Whether a depot is billed as HT IX or LT VIII depends on its connection and is the operator's to confirm. The figures are held in `engine/bus/tariffs.json`; check them against the current order before relying on them for a decision.
